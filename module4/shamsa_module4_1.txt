

What is SQL ?
is (Structured Query Lanugaue) programming lanugaue designed for manage, manipulate,store and retrieve relational databases. 

Why we use it?
 it helps to work with a large amounts of data :
 -create databases and tables
 -Insert new data
 -read (query) data
 -update existing data
 -Delete data 


Who made it?
SQL was developed in the early 1970s by IBM.
The main creators were Donald D. Chamberlin and Raymond F. Boyce.
